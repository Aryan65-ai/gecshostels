Our Story

Government Engineering College, Samastipur

Government Engineering College (GEC), Samastipur was established under the Department of Science & Technology, Government of Bihar, to provide quality engineering education in the region.

The college offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Civil, and Mechanical Engineering with state-of-the-art laboratories and a vibrant campus life.

Our hostel facilities are designed to provide a safe, comfortable, and conducive living environment for students from across Bihar and beyond.

📅 History

Our Journey

Key milestones in GEC Samastipur's growth.

📌

2012 — College Established

GEC Samastipur was founded by the Government of Bihar under the Department of Science & Technology.

🏠

2015 — First Hostel Block

Block A (Boys' Hostel) inaugurated with 60 rooms and 120 student capacity.

🏗️

2018 — Block B Opens

Expansion of boys' hostel with 50 additional rooms in the new Block B.

👩

2020 — Girls' Hostel

Block C (Girls' Hostel) established with modern amenities and enhanced security.

💻

2026 — Digital Portal Launched

Online hostel management system launched for room booking, fee payments, and complaint resolution.
