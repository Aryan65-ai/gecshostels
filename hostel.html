📋 Guidelines

Hostel Rules & Regulations

Ensuring a safe, respectful, and disciplined environment for everyone.

01

All students must carry their ID card inside hostel premises at all times.

02

Entry gate closes at 9:00 PM. Late entry requires warden's permission.

03

Use of electrical appliances (heaters, irons) in rooms is strictly prohibited.

04

Ragging of any kind will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel.

05

Maintain cleanliness in rooms, corridors, and common areas.

06

Visitors are allowed only in the common room during designated hours.

07

Any damage to hostel property must be reported and compensated.
